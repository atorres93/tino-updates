Tino

A small, private browser for macOS 12 Monterey and later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Download Tino 0.1.175

First install

  1. Open the zip (your Mac usually does this for you in Downloads).
  2. Drag Tino to Applications.
  3. Open Tino. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click Open.

After that, Tino updates itself: it checks here once a day, and Tino → Check for Updates… checks now.
